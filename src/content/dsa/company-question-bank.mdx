## How to use this page

Every loop runs the same **funnel** — a coding screen, then onsite rounds that add design:

1. **DSA round** — clear it with the [patterns](/dsa/how-to-think) and the
   [Blind 75](/dsa/blind-75).
2. **LLD round** (SDE-2+) — [OOP/SOLID](/lld/oop-and-solid), patterns, and the classics
   ([LRU](/lld/lru-cache), [vending machine](/lld/vending-machine),
   [tic-tac-toe](/lld/tic-tac-toe)).
3. **HLD round** (SDE-2/3) — the [approach](/hld/approach) plus case studies
   ([URL shortener](/hld/url-shortener), [feed](/hld/design-twitter), and friends).

<Callout type="warn" title="On 'latest asked questions'">
Specific questions rotate and leak quickly go stale; **patterns don't**. What follows is
the durable signal — each company's *style* and the *recurring* question shapes reported
through 2025–2026 — mapped to the pages that train them. Drill the pattern, not the
trophy question.
</Callout>

## By level — what each round expects

- **SDE-1 / new grad:** DSA is ~everything. Easy–medium across arrays, strings, hashing,
  trees, BFS/DFS, basic DP. LLD light (one OOP design), no HLD.
- **SDE-2:** harder DSA (medium–hard), a full **LLD** round, and an entry **HLD** round.
- **SDE-3 / Staff:** DSA still screens, but **HLD depth** and trade-off judgment dominate,
  plus deep LLD and ownership/behavioral.

## Big Tech

### Amazon
- **Style:** medium DSA + a strong **LP/behavioral** bar; OOD is common at SDE-2.
- **DSA:** graphs/BFS-DFS, [heaps & intervals](/dsa/heaps-and-intervals),
  [two pointers](/dsa/two-pointers), [DP](/dsa/dynamic-programming).
- **LLD/HLD:** [parking lot](/lld/parking-lot), [LRU](/lld/lru-cache); design
  [a URL shortener](/hld/url-shortener), [a distributed cache](/hld/distributed-cache).

### Google
- **Style:** algorithmic depth + clean analysis; expect "now optimize / now prove it."
- **DSA:** [graphs](/dsa/advanced-graphs), [DP patterns](/dsa/dp-patterns),
  [backtracking](/dsa/backtracking), [tries](/dsa/tries), [math](/dsa/math-for-coding).
- **HLD:** [typeahead](/hld/typeahead), [web crawler](/hld/web-crawler),
  [YouTube](/hld/design-youtube). Sometimes a [concurrency](/dsa/concurrency-coding) round.

### Meta
- **Style:** fast medium DSA (speed matters), product-flavored HLD, "ownership" behavioral.
- **DSA:** [arrays/strings](/dsa/arrays-and-strings), [graphs](/dsa/graphs-bfs-dfs),
  [intervals](/dsa/heaps-and-intervals), [binary search](/dsa/binary-search).
- **HLD:** [news feed / Twitter](/hld/design-twitter), [Instagram](/hld/design-instagram),
  [ad click aggregation](/hld/ad-click-aggregator).

### Microsoft
- **Style:** balanced, fundamentals-first; OOD and some [bit](/dsa/bit-manipulation) work.
- **DSA:** [linked lists](/dsa/linked-lists), [trees](/dsa/trees-and-bsts),
  [strings](/dsa/string-algorithms), DP.
- **LLD/HLD:** [tic-tac-toe](/lld/tic-tac-toe), [file system](/lld/file-system);
  [distributed ID](/hld/distributed-unique-id).

### Apple / Netflix
- **Apple:** fundamentals + domain depth; [LLD](/lld/oop-and-solid) and memory-aware code.
- **Netflix:** senior bar — [HLD at scale](/hld/scaling-journey),
  [streaming/CDN](/hld/design-netflix), strong system ownership over puzzle DSA.

## Startups & mid-size product companies

Fewer rounds, more "can you ship": one practical DSA screen, often a take-home or a
realistic [LLD](/lld/oop-and-solid), and a pragmatic [HLD](/hld/approach) about *their*
product. Breadth ([backend](/backend/microservices), [databases](/databases/indexing),
[caching](/backend/caching-strategies)) beats exotic algorithms.

<QA q="I have limited time before a loop — how do I prioritize across DSA, LLD, and HLD?">

Match effort to the **round you'll actually get**, which depends on level. New grad / SDE-1:
spend almost everything on **DSA** (the [Blind 75](/dsa/blind-75) patterns) — there's
usually no design round. SDE-2: keep DSA sharp but add a full **LLD** pass (the
[classics](/lld/lru-cache)) and an entry **HLD** ([approach](/hld/approach) +
[URL shortener](/hld/url-shortener)). SDE-3 / Staff: DSA only needs to stay warm; invest in
**HLD depth and trade-offs** plus deep LLD. Then bias toward the specific company's style
from the lists above (e.g. Amazon behavioral/LP, Google analysis, Stripe API design).

</QA>

<QA q="Are leaked 'recently asked' question lists worth grinding?">

Use them as a **distribution**, not a script. Specific questions rotate, get banned, or
won't recur for you, so memorizing a leaked list is brittle — and interviewers probe with
follow-ups that punish memorization. The reliable signal is the **pattern frequency** and
the **company's style** (which this page maps to the training pages). Drill the underlying
pattern from a blank editor until you can re-derive it; that transfers to whatever variant
you're actually handed.

</QA>
